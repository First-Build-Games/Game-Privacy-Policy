Privacy Policy for HatchUp

Last Updated: May 31, 2026

Thank you for playing HatchUp.

HatchUp is a casual puzzle game where players merge eggs to create larger eggs, earn coins, unlock egg skins, and customize their gameplay experience.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected and used when you use the game.

Information We Collect

HatchUp does not require users to create an account and does not directly collect personal information such as:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Home address
  • Payment information
  • Government-issued identification

We do not maintain user accounts and do not store personal information on our own servers.

Advertising

HatchUp may display advertisements, including:

  • Banner advertisements
  • Interstitial advertisements
  • Rewarded advertisements

Advertisements are provided by third-party advertising partners, such as Google AdMob.

Advertising services may automatically collect certain information to provide, measure, and improve advertising services, including:

  • Device information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • IP address
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Ad interaction information
  • Diagnostic and performance information

Consent for Advertising

For users located in regions where consent is required by law, such as the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, HatchUp may display a consent form before showing advertisements.

Users may manage their advertising preferences through the options provided in the consent form where applicable.

Firebase Services

HatchUp uses Firebase services provided by Google, including Firebase Remote Config, to manage certain game settings and features.

Depending on the Firebase services enabled, limited technical information may be processed to ensure proper operation, reliability, and performance of the game.

Internet Connection

An internet connection is not required for core gameplay.

However, an internet connection may be required for:

  • Loading advertisements
  • Updating remote configuration settings
  • Accessing certain online services used by the game

If no internet connection is available, advertisements may not be shown and some online features may be unavailable.

Third-Party Services

HatchUp may use third-party services including:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Firebase
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

For more information about how Google handles information, please visit:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children's Privacy

HatchUp is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that personal information has been provided by a child in violation of applicable laws, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to help protect the integrity and security of the game and its associated services. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

Any updates will be posted on this page and the "Last Updated" date will be revised accordingly.
